## Ground rules before anyone submits
- Use a brand role mailbox on our own domain, never a personal address. Several forms (SaaSHub, Uneed, Launching Next) ask for an email; SaaSHub also verifies by an address on the product domain.
- SaaSHub says an AI agent must tell its human to submit. Treat every form as human-submitted.
- Personal-account sites (Product Hunt, Peerlist, Indie Hackers) put a named person on the listing. That conflicts with the anonymous-brand rule, so they are the owner's call, ranked low.
- Shopli earns affiliate commission. AlternativeTo's terms ban "advertising or affiliate links disguised as recommendations" [search], so the listing text says plainly that Shopli earns a commission. No directory read so far bans an affiliate-funded free tool outright, but PH reportedly does not feature stores and deal sites [search, secondary source].
- Submit once per directory, vary the opening line, never pay for "link packages".

## Dropped
- BetaList: its FAQ says "All submissions are paid. There is no free submission option." [read]
- Fazier dofollow, Microlaunch deals, TinyLaunch premium, Uneed fast-track, Launching Next $99: paid upgrades only, skipped.
- Coupon and deal sites (RetailMeNot, Slickdeals, CouponFollow): built for merchant codes and community deal posts, not for listing an affiliate tracker; no free "list my site" page found [search]. Not recommended.
- Israeli and Hebrew directories: two searches (English and Hebrew) found no live Hebrew site directory or Hebrew Telegram catalog with a free submit form. science.co.il only takes science and tech sites by contact form [search]. Nothing worth submitting; Hebrew copy below is for TGStat (language field) and any Hebrew field found later.
- DevHunt, TLDR-style tool lists, AI tool lists: Shopli is not a developer or AI tool, so poor fit.
- Reddit: not a directory, skipped as asked.

## Suggested order
1. TGStat (5 min, Telegram channel). 2. AlternativeTo (long queue, so start it first). 3. SaaSHub. 4. Uneed and Launching Next. 5. Telemetr and Telegramic once a human has opened their forms. 6. Leave 8+ unless the first six show referral visits in Growth Radar after 30 days.

**Name:** Shopli

**Tagline (56 chars):** Check if an AliExpress price drop is real before you buy
**Short description (141 chars):** Shopli tracks AliExpress prices and order counts every day, so you can tell a real price drop from a fake one. Free, no signup, in 8 regions.

(Eight regions = Israel, US, UK, EU, FR, DE, ES, IT, as stated in the brief.)

**Long description (English, 558 chars):**
Shopli is a free website for people who shop on AliExpress and want to know whether a discount is real. It records each product's price and order count every day and gives it a simple verdict: good price, or wait. You can compare products and browse curated collections. Shoppers in Israel get an import calculator for VAT and customs on orders under $75, and the site is available in Hebrew. There are also versions for the US, UK, EU, France, Germany, Spain and Italy. No account needed. Shopli earns a commission when you buy through its AliExpress links.
